The operating gap

Business systems record operations. DecisionOS connects the context needed to understand what happened and decide what to do next.

ERP, CRM, project-management, and analytics systems provide valuable records and decision support. But records alone rarely capture which constraint mattered, why an exception was made, what alternatives existed, who exercised judgment, and what happened afterward.

DecisionOS connects those records with the context, relationships, decisions, and outcomes that explain them.

Planned flow
Order → Production → Delivery
Actual execution
Order change → Material constraint → Manager decision → Production adjustment → Delivery change → Outcome

What it does

Four capabilities. One decision system.

01
Connects operating context

Understands the company's systems, terminology, roles, workflows, relationships, constraints, objectives, and evidence—and keeps that understanding current.

02
Reconstructs decisions

Connects events, evidence, alternatives, human judgment, actions, and outcomes—not merely documents and database rows.

03
Surfaces divergence

Shows where expected processes and actual execution differ, including exceptions, bottlenecks, changing constraints, and hidden dependencies.

04
Improves future execution

Uses previous decisions and outcomes to improve context, recommendations, evaluation, and reusable organizational knowledge.
